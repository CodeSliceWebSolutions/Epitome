STEP 1 — Shift Attention Away from Self

Goal: Curiosity begins where self-monitoring ends.

Practice (Daily – every interaction)

Before engaging, say internally: “This person is more interesting than my thoughts about myself.” Then direct attention outward.

Measurement:

Reduced self-consciousness Less inner narration Increased ease in conversations

✅ Mastery indicator: You stop wondering how you’re coming across.
